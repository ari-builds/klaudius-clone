Frequently asked questions

How does Klaudius decide which businesses to target?
You provide a region and optional niche. Klaudius searches for businesses without websites, or with outdated ones or booking-platform-only pages, and ranks them by how likely they are to buy.
Can I customise the websites and how Klaudius works?
Yes. Klaudius is highly agnostic — tell it in plain English to swap your design system, target a niche, change outreach wording, or wire in your own tools.
What AI does Klaudius use?
Klaudius runs on your own computer with your own Claude or ChatGPT subscription. Everything else runs on free tiers at a normal pace.
Is there a mobile app?
Yes — the Klaudius mobile app for iPhone and Android comes with every plan. Start runs, answer Klaudius, and send follow-ups from anywhere.
What does a single website sale pay for?
One sale typically pays for months of Klaudius. The platform is built for volume — multiple professional websites a day.
