Klaudius

A small, honest tool for building site previews.

Klaudius is a personal dashboard for turning business details you supply into private one-page previews, preparing pitch drafts you review yourself, and keeping track of what still needs a human decision.

What it does

  • Generates a private preview from details you actually entered.
  • Drafts short outreach text using only the business name, supplied services and the preview URL.
  • Keeps a review queue for previews and drafts, with approval and manual-send confirmation steps.

What it does not do

  • It does not discover or verify businesses for you.
  • It does not send email, make calls, or reply on your behalf.
  • It does not publish a site until you explicitly approve it.
